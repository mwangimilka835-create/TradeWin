How to read this

Volatility indices come from a random number generator. How often a digit or direction showed up recently does not change the odds of the next tick, and a digit that looks "due" is not. Use this to describe recent behavior and to sanity-check a bot's settings, not to predict the next tick. The χ² line tells you if the digit pattern is more uneven than chance alone usually produces.
